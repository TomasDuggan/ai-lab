# IT & Security Policy

## Overview
This document covers device usage, password requirements, and access
control for company systems.

## Rules

- All company-issued devices must have full-disk encryption and
  automatic screen lock (5 minutes of inactivity) enabled at all times.
- Passwords must be at least 12 characters, rotated every 90 days, and
  may not be reused within the last 5 password cycles. Multi-factor
  authentication is required for all systems handling client data.
- Production system access is granted on a role basis and reviewed
  quarterly. See the Access Control Policy for the full approval
  workflow and role-to-permission mapping.
- Employees working internationally for more than 30 consecutive days
  (see Remote Work Policy) must use company-approved VPN software at all
  times when accessing internal systems, regardless of the destination
  country's data regulations.
- Personal devices may access email and calendar only, never production
  systems or client data, under any circumstance.

## Edge cases / exceptions

- Contractors are issued time-limited access credentials that expire
  automatically at the end of their contract term, rather than following
  the quarterly review cycle that applies to employees.
- A lost or stolen device must be reported to IT within 2 hours of
  discovery. Failure to report within 24 hours is treated as a Moderate
  violation under the Code of Conduct, regardless of whether any data
  was actually compromised.
- Emergency production access outside the normal quarterly review
  process may be granted for up to 24 hours by a Director, but must be
  retroactively documented and reviewed within 48 hours.
