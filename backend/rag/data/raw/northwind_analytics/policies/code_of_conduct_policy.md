# Code of Conduct

## Overview
This document defines expected employee behavior, prohibited conduct,
and the disciplinary process for violations.

## Rules

- Prohibited conduct includes: harassment or discrimination of any kind,
  falsification of company records (including expense reports - see
  Expense Reimbursement Policy), unauthorized disclosure of confidential
  information, and misuse of company systems (see IT & Security Policy).
- Violations are classified into three severity tiers:
  - **Minor** (e.g. repeated lateness, dress code violations): verbal
    warning, documented in the employee's file.
  - **Moderate** (e.g. policy violations, insubordination): written
    warning and a formal improvement plan.
  - **Severe** (e.g. harassment, theft, falsification of records):
    immediate suspension pending investigation, up to and including
    termination.
- Employees have the right to have a peer or HR representative present
  during any disciplinary meeting related to a Moderate or Severe
  violation.
- All reports of misconduct are handled confidentially by HR, and
  retaliation against an employee who files a report in good faith is
  itself classified as a Severe violation.

## Edge cases / exceptions

- A Minor violation does not escalate to Moderate automatically, unless
  three or more Minor violations occur within a rolling 6-month period,
  in which case the fourth is treated as Moderate regardless of its
  individual severity.
- Conduct occurring outside of work hours or company premises may still
  be subject to this policy if it directly affects the employee's
  ability to perform their role or creates a hostile environment for
  colleagues, at HR's discretion.
- Contractors are held to the confidentiality and harassment provisions
  of this policy but are not subject to the tiered warning system -
  violations by contractors are handled through contract termination
  directly.
