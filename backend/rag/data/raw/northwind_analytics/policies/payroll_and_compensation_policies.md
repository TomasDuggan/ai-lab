# Payroll & Compensation Policy

## Overview
This document covers pay schedules, overtime calculation, and bonus
structures for all employees.

## Rules

- Employees are paid on a bi-weekly schedule, every other Friday. If a
  payday falls on a public holiday, payment is issued the preceding
  business day.
- Non-exempt employees (hourly-classified roles) are paid overtime at
  1.5x their base hourly rate for any hours worked beyond 40 in a single
  week. Exempt (salaried) employees are not eligible for overtime pay
  under any circumstance.
- Annual performance bonuses are calculated as a percentage of base
  salary, tied to the employee's most recent Performance Review Policy
  rating:
  - "Exceeds expectations": up to 15% of base salary
  - "Meets expectations": up to 8% of base salary
  - "Below expectations": not eligible for bonus that cycle
- Salary reviews occur annually, aligned with the performance review
  cycle. Off-cycle salary adjustments require VP approval and are
  reserved for role changes or retention cases.

## Edge cases / exceptions

- Employees who transfer between exempt and non-exempt classification
  mid-pay-period have their pay for that period calculated on a
  split basis - the exempt portion and non-exempt (with overtime, if
  applicable) portion are calculated separately, not averaged.
- New hires starting mid-pay-period receive a prorated first paycheck
  based on actual days worked, not a full period's pay.
- Bonus eligibility requires the employee to be actively employed on the
  bonus payout date, except in the case of an approved medical or
  parental leave (see Parental Leave Policy), where eligibility is
  preserved.
