# Performance Review Policy

## Overview
This document covers review frequency, rating criteria, and how ratings
connect to compensation and other policies.

## Rules

- Formal performance reviews occur annually, aligned with the
  Payroll & Compensation Policy salary review cycle. New hires receive
  an additional informal review at the 90-day probation mark.
- Ratings fall into three tiers: "Exceeds expectations," "Meets
  expectations," and "Below expectations." Ratings are determined by the
  employee's direct manager, with input from at least one peer reviewer
  for roles at Director level or above.
- Employees rated "Below expectations" are placed on a formal 60-day
  improvement plan. Failure to show documented improvement by the end of
  the 60 days may result in termination under the Termination &
  Resignation Policy.
- Review ratings directly determine annual bonus eligibility, as
  described in the Payroll & Compensation Policy, and may affect Remote
  Work Policy eligibility at manager discretion.

## Edge cases / exceptions

- An employee may formally dispute a rating within 10 business days of
  receiving it. During an active dispute, no policy consequence tied to
  that rating (bonus reduction, remote work suspension) takes effect
  until the dispute is resolved.
- Employees on an approved leave of absence during the review period
  (medical, parental, etc.) have their review postponed until 30 days
  after their return, rather than being rated based on a partial year.
- Peer reviewer feedback is considered advisory only for
  Director-level-and-above reviews - the final rating decision always
  rests with the employee's direct manager, not the peer reviewer.
