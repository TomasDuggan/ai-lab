# Onboarding Process

## Overview
This document covers the first-week checklist, probation period terms,
and role classification for new hires.

## Rules

- All new hires undergo a 90-day probation period, during which either
  party may terminate employment with 5 business days' notice, without
  the standard notice period described in the Termination & Resignation
  Policy.
- Roles are classified at time of hire as either "office-required" or
  "remote-eligible" - this classification determines Remote Work Policy
  eligibility once probation ends.
- New hires receive company equipment (laptop, access badge, required
  software licenses) before their start date whenever logistically
  possible, and no later than end of day on day 1.
- A 30-60-90 day check-in is scheduled with the employee's manager and
  an HR representative, to review progress against onboarding goals.
- New hires are not eligible to use PTO during their first 30 days (see
  PTO / Vacation Policy), but do begin accruing PTO and sick leave from
  their start date.

## Edge cases / exceptions

- Employees transferring internally from another team or role do not
  undergo a new 90-day probation period, unless the transfer includes a
  change from individual contributor to a people-management role.
- If a new hire's start date falls within the last two weeks of a fiscal
  quarter (the PTO blackout period), the 90-day probation clock still
  starts on their actual start date and is not delayed.
- Contractors converting to full-time employment retain their prior
  tenure for PTO accrual-tier purposes (see PTO / Vacation Policy) only
  if the conversion happens within 30 days of the contract ending;
  otherwise tenure resets to zero.
