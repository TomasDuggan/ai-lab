# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Overview
This document covers what business expenses are reimbursable, approval
limits by role, and submission deadlines.

## Rules

- Reimbursable expenses include: client meals, business travel
  (see Travel Policy for per-diem details), software subscriptions
  approved for the employee's role, and home office supplies up to
  $150/year.
- All expenses must be submitted through the finance portal within
  30 days of the purchase date. Expenses submitted after 30 days require
  Director approval to be processed, and are not guaranteed reimbursement.
- Expenses require manager approval before reimbursement is issued.
  Approval limits by role are as follows:

| Role     | Approval limit (per expense)  |
|----------|-------------------------------|
| Manager  | $1,000                        |
| Director | $5,000                        |
| VP       | $25,000                       |

- Any single expense exceeding a role's approval limit must be escalated
  to the next level up, in addition to the employee's direct manager.
- Personal expenses (e.g. entertainment unrelated to client work,
  personal phone bills) are not reimbursable under any circumstance.

## Edge cases / exceptions

- Expenses related to conferences or training require pre-approval from
  the employee's manager *before* the purchase is made, unlike other
  categories which are approved after the fact.
- Contractors follow a separate reimbursement process defined in their
  individual agreements and are not covered by the approval limits table
  above.
- If an expense report includes a mix of reimbursable and
  non-reimbursable items, only the reimbursable portion is processed;
  the report is not rejected in full unless more than 50% of the total
  amount is non-reimbursable.
