# Travel Policy

## Overview
This document covers trip approval, per-diem rates, and flight/hotel
booking rules for business travel.

## Rules

- All business travel requires manager pre-approval before booking,
  regardless of cost - unlike standard expenses (see Expense
  Reimbursement Policy), which are approved after the fact.
- Domestic per-diem for meals not covered by a client or event is $75/day.
  International per-diem is $110/day, adjusted by finance for
  high-cost-of-living destinations on a case-by-case basis.
- Flight class eligibility is based on role and flight duration:

| Role                   | Flights under 6 hours  | Flights 6+ hours  |
|------------------------|------------------------|-------------------|
| Individual Contributor | Economy                | Economy           |
| Manager                | Economy                | Premium Economy   |
| Director and above     | Premium Economy        | Business          |

- Hotel bookings should not exceed $250/night domestically or
  $350/night internationally, without Director approval for the excess.

## Edge cases / exceptions

- Employees may use personal frequent flyer miles or hotel loyalty
  points for business travel and are not required to disclose or
  surrender any rewards earned, even though the company paid for the
  ticket or stay.
- Travel extended for personal reasons (e.g. staying an extra weekend)
  is permitted, but any additional cost - including per-diem for the
  extended days - is the employee's responsibility, not the company's.
- In the case of a trip cancellation due to a documented medical
  emergency (see Sick Leave & Medical Policy), non-refundable booking
  costs are covered by the company and do not count against the
  employee's expense approval limit described in the Expense
  Reimbursement Policy.
