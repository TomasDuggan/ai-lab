# Termination & Resignation Policy

## Overview
This document covers notice periods, final pay, and offboarding
requirements for voluntary and involuntary separations.

## Rules

- Employees resigning voluntarily must provide 2 weeks' written notice
  for individual contributor roles, and 4 weeks' notice for Manager
  level and above, submitted to their direct manager and HR.
- Involuntary termination for performance reasons requires completion of
  the improvement plan process described in the Performance Review
  Policy, except in cases of a Severe violation under the Code of
  Conduct, which allows for immediate termination without a prior
  improvement plan.
- Final pay, including any accrued and unused PTO (see PTO / Vacation
  Policy), is issued within 5 business days of the separation date,
  or sooner if required by local law.
- All company equipment (laptop, access badge, phone) must be returned
  on or before the last working day. Failure to return equipment within
  10 business days may result in its cost being deducted from final pay,
  where legally permitted.

## Edge cases / exceptions

- Employees terminated during their 90-day probation period (see
  Onboarding Process) are not subject to the standard notice period
  requirements in either direction.
- An employee who resigns and later requests to rescind that resignation
  may do so only with manager approval, and only before the resignation
  has been formally processed by HR - once processed, rescission is
  treated as a new hire application.
- Unused sick leave is not paid out upon separation, per the Sick Leave
  & Medical Policy, regardless of the reason for termination or
  resignation - this is a key contrast with PTO payout treatment.
