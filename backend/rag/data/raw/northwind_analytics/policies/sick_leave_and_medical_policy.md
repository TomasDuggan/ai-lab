# Sick Leave & Medical Policy

## Overview
This document covers paid sick leave, medical certification
requirements, and extended medical leave, separate from PTO.

## Rules

- Full-time employees receive 10 paid sick days per calendar year,
  separate from PTO balance (see PTO / Vacation Policy). Sick days do
  not accrue monthly - the full 10 days are available starting January
  1st, or on the employee's start date if hired mid-year (pro-rated).
- A medical certificate is required for any sick leave of 3 or more
  consecutive days. For 1-2 day absences, a manager notification is
  sufficient - no certificate is required.
- Unused sick days do not carry over to the following year and are not
  paid out upon resignation or termination, unlike PTO.
- Extended medical leave (beyond the 10-day sick leave allowance)
  requires a formal leave of absence request through HR, and follows the
  accrual-pause rules described in the PTO / Vacation Policy for
  leaves beyond 90 days.

## Edge cases / exceptions

- Sick days used for a dependent's medical needs (child, spouse, or
  registered domestic partner) are covered under this same policy, up to
  5 of the 10 annual days, unless local law provides broader family
  leave coverage.
- An employee who exhausts their 10 sick days may draw from their PTO
  balance instead, at their discretion, without needing separate
  approval - this is the one case where sick leave and PTO balances are
  interchangeable.
- Sick leave taken during an already-approved PTO period does not
  convert that day back to PTO, except when a medical certificate is
  provided within 5 business days of returning to work.
