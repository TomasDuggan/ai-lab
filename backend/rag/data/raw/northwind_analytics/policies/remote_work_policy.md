# Remote Work Policy

## Overview
This document covers eligibility, equipment, and expectations for
employees working remotely, either fully remote or in a hybrid
arrangement.

## Rules

- Employees are eligible for hybrid work (up to 3 days/week remote)
  after completing their 90-day probation period, unless their role is
  designated as office-required (see Onboarding Process for role
  classifications).
- Fully remote arrangements require Director-level approval and are
  reviewed annually.
- Remote employees must be available during a 4-hour "core overlap
  window" aligned with their team's primary timezone, even if their
  full schedule is flexible outside that window.
- Company-issued equipment (laptop, monitor, headset) is provided for
  all remote employees. A one-time home office stipend of $500 is
  available within the first 60 days of a remote arrangement being
  approved.
- Employees working from a different country than their contract's
  country of record for more than 30 consecutive days must notify HR in
  advance, due to tax and legal implications - see the IT & Security
  Policy for device compliance requirements when working
  internationally.

## Edge cases / exceptions

- Contractors are not eligible for the home office stipend, regardless
  of remote work arrangement, except when explicitly stated in their
  individual contract.
- Employees may work remotely from a non-primary location (e.g. while
  traveling) for up to 10 business days per year without formal
  approval, unless the destination requires the international-work
  notification described above.
- An employee whose performance rating falls below "meets expectations"
  (see Performance Review Policy) may have remote eligibility suspended
  at manager discretion until the next review cycle, except when the
  rating is under formal dispute.
