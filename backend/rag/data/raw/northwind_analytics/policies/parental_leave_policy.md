# Parental Leave Policy

## Overview
This document covers leave eligibility and duration for childbirth,
adoption, and foster placement, for all parenting roles.

## Rules

- Employees with 6+ months of continuous employment are eligible for
  parental leave. Employees with less than 6 months of tenure are only
  eligible for unpaid leave under this policy, unless local law requires
  otherwise.
- Birthing parents receive 16 weeks of fully paid leave. Non-birthing
  parents (including in cases of adoption or foster placement) receive
  8 weeks of fully paid leave.
- Leave may be taken continuously or split into two blocks within the
  first 12 months following the birth or placement, but not split into
  more than two blocks without HR approval.
- Parental leave accrual-pause rules for PTO follow the same 90-day
  threshold described in the PTO / Vacation Policy.

## Edge cases / exceptions

- In the case of multiple births (twins, triplets, etc.), leave duration
  is not extended beyond the standard 16/8 week allowance, except when
  a documented medical complication requires additional recovery time -
  in that case, the excess is covered under Sick Leave & Medical Policy
  provisions, not parental leave.
- Employees who separate from the company within 6 months of returning
  from parental leave are not required to repay any portion of the paid
  leave received, unlike some signing bonuses or relocation packages.
- Same-sex couples and domestic partners are eligible under the same
  terms as married couples; eligibility is not contingent on legal
  marital status.
